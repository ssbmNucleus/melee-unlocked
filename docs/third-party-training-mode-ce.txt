Training Mode - Community Edition

Nucleus runs Training Mode CE natively, as part of the game: on the 1-P menu, the Event Match row
reads "Training Mode" and opens Training Mode CE's event menu (the Training Lab and the drills).
Its C source is compiled into melee_game.dll, and the game-side changes its PowerPC patches make
are written as C at the same points in the game's source. Its data files (the TM folder beside
the game: eventMenu.dat, lab.dat, labCSS.dat and wavedash.dat) are upstream's own dats/ files,
unchanged, taken by the game's build from the same commit and checked against the hash recorded
in sourceport/game/tmce/upstream.txt.

Training Mode CE by Aitch (Alexander Harrison) and contributors: an expanded and updated version
of UnclePunch's training modpack for Super Smash Bros. Melee.
